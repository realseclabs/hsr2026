About the run · Season 2

Run along the
coastal way.

After an incredible first edition, Haleyangadi Sea Walk Run is back with a scenic running experience by the Arabian Sea. Follow the Sea Walk through the beautiful Haleyangadi coastline, with open skies, ocean views and the sound of the waves beside you.

In collaboration with Surathkal Beach Runners (SBR), Season 2 welcomes seasoned runners, weekend runners and anyone taking their first step into an organised run. Come for the challenge, stay for the view, and make every mile memorable.

The event is presented in association with the Sri Vidya Vinayaka Yuvaka Mandala (R.), Haleyangadi and the Sri Vidya Vinayaka Rajatha Seva Trust (R.), Haleyangadi, with the support of the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India, and the Youth Service & Sports Department, My Bharat Dakshina Kannada District.

VIDYA VINAYAKA · HALEYANGADI

The organizer

About
Vidya Vinayaka
Yuvaka Mandala.

Vidya Vinayaka Yuvaka Mandala, Haleyangadi, established on 6 October 1970, is a youth organisation dedicated to social service, education, sports, culture and community development.

For over five decades, it has nurtured unity, discipline and responsible youth participation in the spirit of “Sangha Shakti – Rashtra Shakti” (the strength of the organisation is the strength of the nation).

A legacy of youth, service and community spirit since 1970.
SBR · SURATHKAL · MANGALURU

The team behind the run

About
SBR.

Surathkal Beach Runners (SBR) is a community-driven running club in Surathkal, Mangaluru, bringing runners together through regular runs, shared experiences and a passion for running.

From short beach runs to long-distance training, SBR creates a supportive environment where runners can stay consistent, challenge themselves and grow together, while encouraging participation in events and community initiatives.

Running together. Growing together.
